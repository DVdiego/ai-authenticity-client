# Notice

AI Authenticity Client is an academic prototype developed in the context of a university TFG.

## Intended context

- research and educational use
- internal demos
- controlled evaluation of image authenticity indicators

## Important limitations

- the software can be wrong
- false positives and false negatives are possible
- outputs are advisory only
- no guarantee is made that a result is suitable for moderation, forensic, compliance, legal, or
